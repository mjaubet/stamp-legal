Privacy Policy — Stamp

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 7Lakes LLC ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Stamp (the "App").

Summary

Stamp does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us or to any third party. All data the App uses stays on your device.

Information the App stores locally on your device

To remember your preferences between play sessions, the App saves a small settings file on your own device only. This includes:

  • Gameplay preferences (dominant hand for the emergency button, difficulty level)
  • Sound, music, and vibration on/off settings
  • An age range you select during first-time setup (used only to suggest a difficulty default — not linked to any identity, never leaves your device)
  • Your local high score

None of this information is transmitted anywhere, is not linked to you personally, and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the App removes it.

Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It does not use accounts, logins, or in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The age range mentioned above is a coarse, optional, locally-stored preference, not personal data collection.

Changes to this policy

If Stamp adds new features that change how information is handled (for example, online leaderboards in a future update), this policy will be updated accordingly before that feature is released.
